My Tackle Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

My Tackle Box is built so your fishing spots stay yours.

What stays on your phone

Your catch log, saved spots, tackle box, agreement to these terms and cached conditions are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive them, and there are no accounts. Pattern Match, which compares today's conditions to your log, runs entirely on your phone.

Location

When you allow it, My Tackle Box uses your location on your device to find the water you are fishing. The app asks for location only while you are using it. To look up conditions, the app sends coordinates (never your name, email or a device identifier) to these public data services:

  • Open-Meteo: weather, ocean temperature and waves
  • U.S. Geological Survey: water maps, water temperature and river flow
  • NOAA CO-OPS: tides and coastal water temperature
  • National Weather Service: weather alerts and the state you are in
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: place search, only when you type a search

Those services receive the request the way any website receives a visit, including your network address, and their own privacy policies apply. We do not receive or store these requests.

No tracking

My Tackle Box has no ads, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in California law.

Shop links

Some lure links go to retailers such as Tackle Warehouse and Amazon and may include an affiliate tag so we can earn a commission. The tag identifies us, not you. Links open in your browser, and anything you do there is covered by that retailer's privacy policy.

Subscriptions

If you buy My Tackle Box Pro, Apple or Google processes the payment. We never see your payment details. To confirm your subscription, the app may use a subscription service (RevenueCat) that receives an anonymous app user ID and your store purchase receipt. It does not receive your name or location. This section takes effect only once Pro is offered.

Deleting your data

Log, then About, then Delete all my data removes everything My Tackle Box stored on your phone. Deleting the app does the same. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Children

My Tackle Box is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: you can view, export (Log, then Export) and delete it at any time. California residents may contact us with any privacy question and will not be treated differently for doing so.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
