Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone only. Log, then Export saves a copy as a spreadsheet file.

How do I delete my data? Log, then About, then Delete all my data.

How do I cancel Pro? On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

The water type is wrong. Tap Not right? under the water name and pick the right one.
